Zeus

A private assistant for one household.

Zeus helps the people in one household with their mail, calendar, files and reminders, through chat messages. It runs on the household's own computer. It is not a public service and does not take sign-ups.

Google sign-in lets each household member allow Zeus to work with their own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive. Each person decides what to allow, and can take that permission back at any time.
