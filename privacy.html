Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Zeus is a private assistant run by one household on its own computer. It is not a public service: only members of that household use it, and each of them signs in with Google themselves.

What Zeus can access

When you sign in, Google shows you what Zeus asks for, and you choose what to allow:

  • Gmail: read your mail (read-only). If you also allow sending, Zeus sends an email only after you approve that exact draft.
  • Google Calendar: see events on calendars you own, and add, change or delete them when you ask.
  • Google Drive: read your files and create new ones. Zeus never edits, deletes, moves or shares your existing files.

How it is used

  • Only to do what you ask: answer your questions, summarise mail, add calendar events, and send the reminders you set up.
  • To answer a request, the text needed for it is sent to the AI model service that powers Zeus (OpenAI's API).
  • Your data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising.

Where it is kept

  • Your Google sign-in and the things Zeus keeps for you (reminders, notes, and some conversation history for up to 180 days) stay on the household's own computer.
  • Each person's sign-in is kept separately and used only for that person's requests.

Your choices

  • Remove Zeus's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page.
  • Ask the household member who runs Zeus to delete what it keeps about you.

Google API data

Zeus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
